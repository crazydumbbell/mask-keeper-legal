Mask Keeper data deletion request

Developer: exchip · App: Mask Keeper - Idle RPG (com.exchip.maskidle)

The game has no accounts. Game progress is stored only on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

How to request deletion

  1. Email wogus5357@gmail.com with the subject "Mask Keeper data deletion".
  2. Include the store you bought from (Google Play or App Store) and your order number, or the purchase date if you do not have it.
  3. We process requests within 30 days and reply by email.

Data that is deleted

  • Purchase records and the anonymous user ID held by RevenueCat.
  • Google AdMob ad records: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in device settings. Retention of records held by AdMob follows Google's policy.

Data that is kept

  • Order and payment records held by Google Play and Apple follow each store's policy and legal retention periods (up to 5 years under Korean e-commerce law). We cannot delete them.
